Paper / long-document reading

A PDF reader for long documents.

Long reading needs a stable place to return to, a line that does not exhaust you, and an interface quiet enough to disappear.

Paper is especially useful when a PDF asks for sustained attention: a book, report, thesis, paper, or essay. The same reading surface also works for a short document when the default viewer gets in the way.

Built for the session, not just the file

Long documents are hard to read online because small frictions compound. Tiny type, wide lines, repeated page furniture, and lost position all make it easier to stop. Paper gives the text a responsive column and lets the browser remember your place.

Comfort over time

Set text size, line width, spacing, and theme for the screen and room you are actually reading in.

Progress in the browser

Your position is stored locally, without an account or a server-side library of your documents.

Text-first structure

Paper reconstructs headings and paragraphs so the document can be followed as a reading stream.

A source you can check

The original PDF link stays important for citations, figures, exact pages, and visual verification.

Currently supported formats

Paper currently supports public HTTP(S) URLs to selectable-text PDFs up to 30 MB. It does not yet support scanned PDFs, OCR, EPUB, or standard book links.

Questions

Does Paper support very large PDFs?

The current PDF fetch limit is 30 MB. A document also needs enough selectable text for the reader to reconstruct it.

Is Paper a replacement for a full PDF editor?

No. It is a focused reading view, not an editor, annotator, form filler, or document-management system.
